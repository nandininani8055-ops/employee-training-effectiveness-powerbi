# Employee Training Effectiveness Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze employee training effectiveness across **5,000 training records**.

The dashboard evaluates training completion, employee score improvement, satisfaction, training costs, and program performance to provide meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall training completion rates
* Measure improvement in employee scores after training
* Evaluate employee satisfaction
* Track training expenditure
* Compare training effectiveness across departments and programs
* Identify training completion and drop-off patterns
* Analyze the relationship between training cost and score improvement

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains **5,000 employee training records** with 10 columns:

* EmployeeID
* Department
* Region
* TrainingProgram
* EnrollmentDate
* CompletionStatus
* PreTrainingScore
* PostTrainingScore
* TrainingCost_INR
* SatisfactionRating

The dataset covers training activity from **2024–2025**.
